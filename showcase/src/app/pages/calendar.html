Calendar

v1.1 · Phosphor-only — Bootstrap ships no datepicker, so ph-calendar is built from scratch on the token system: a date field that opens a popover with a month grid. BEM-lite: .ph-calendar, __field, __input, __toggle, __popover, __header, __nav, __title, __grid, __day, __footer. Here the open/close, month math, and key handling are Angular signals — the exact JS the CSS expects.

Date field + popover

Drive it from the keyboard: click the field or press ↓/Enter to open, arrows move the day cursor (up/down jump weeks), PgUp/PgDn change month, Home/End jump to the week edge, Enter/Space picks, Esc closes and returns focus.

Static month grid (no popover)
October 2026
Su Mo Tu We Th Fr Sa

States: --outside (adjacent month, muted), --today (amber hairline ring), --selected (solid amber, aria-pressed="true"), --range (between two picks). One day carries tabindex="0", the rest -1 — roving tabindex.

Markup contract

CSS only paints — open/close, month math and the key bindings are your JS (no Bootstrap datepicker involved). Structure: 

<div class="ph-calendar">
  <div class="ph-calendar__field">
    <input class="form-control ph-calendar__input" value="2026-10-13"
           aria-haspopup="dialog" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="cal-pop">
    <button class="ph-calendar__toggle" aria-expanded="false"
            aria-controls="cal-pop" aria-label="Open calendar">▾</button>
  </div>
  <div class="ph-calendar__popover" id="cal-pop" role="dialog" aria-label="Choose date">
    <div class="ph-calendar__header">
      <button class="ph-calendar__nav" aria-label="Previous month">‹</button>
      <span class="ph-calendar__title" aria-live="polite">October 2026</span>
      <button class="ph-calendar__nav" aria-label="Next month">›</button>
    </div>
    <table class="ph-calendar__grid"> … 7 × <th scope="col">, day <button class="ph-calendar__day"> …
    <div class="ph-calendar__footer"> … Today / Clear (.btn .btn-sm) … </div>
  </div>
</div>
